Six centuries of community in India

Around 600 years ago, a missionary named Pir Sadruddin arrived in Sind, in India. There are a number of myths about his origins.

The most common view among historians is that he was a Dai, a representative or emissary, of the Nizari branch of the Ismaili sect. Some have suggested that he was a Sufi teacher from Iran.
