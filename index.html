History

Six hundred years ago a missionary named Pir Sadruddin arrived in Sind. Today the Jamat looks after the families of Mumbai.

600

Historians generally hold that he was a Dai, an emissary of the Nizari branch of the Ismaili sect. Some suggest he was a Sufi teacher from Iran.
